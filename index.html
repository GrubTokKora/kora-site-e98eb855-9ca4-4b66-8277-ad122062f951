Good to know

Questions, answered

Still unsure which treatment is right for you? Call or text Vanessa at (860) 838-7039.

Which treatment should I book first?

If you have a specific concern like breakouts, dark spots or texture, choose the matching facial or peel; each one starts with a personal consultation. If you'd like guidance on your routine first, book a virtual consultation.

What should I expect during a facial?

Expect a personalized consultation followed by a pampering treatment that leaves your skin refreshed and rejuvenated.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with a clean face, and feel free to share any skin concerns so your treatment can be fully customized.

What aftercare do you recommend?

Gentle cleansing and moisturizing, plus avoiding heavy makeup and sun exposure right after your treatment.

Do you work with darker skin tones?

Yes. Vanessa treats every skin type and tone, and many clients choose her specifically for her experience with melanin-rich skin, hyperpigmentation and acne.

Is there parking, and is the studio accessible?

Valet parking is available, and the studio is wheelchair accessible.

Which payments do you accept?

Cash, credit cards and digital wallets. You can also pay over time with Cherry.
