About Vanessa

You are not a standard protocol.

I’m Vanessa. Up on the 22nd floor of 200 Columbus Blvd there’s a quiet room scented with essential oils, and for the length of your appointment, it’s just the two of us.

Every treatment starts with your skin, not a script. I look, I ask, and I build the facial around what I find, using eco-friendly products chosen for what your skin actually needs. No two clients get the same hour from me, because no two clients have the same skin.

I take particular care with melanin-rich skin. It deserves someone who understands how it responds and how it heals, and that understanding shapes every treatment I choose for you.

I want you to leave with skin that looks better, and with a moment of real peace behind you. Both matter to me.

Your first visit

What to know before you come in.

Anything else on your mind? Call me on (860) 838-7039.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with a clean face. Tell me about any skin concerns you have, so I can tailor the treatment to you.

What should I expect during a facial?

I start with a personal consultation about your skin, then move into a treatment chosen for it. You’ll leave with skin that feels refreshed and renewed.

What aftercare do you recommend?

Cleanse gently and moisturize. Afterwards, avoid heavy makeup and keep out of the sun.

Is the spa accessible?

Yes. My spa is wheelchair accessible, and everyone is welcome.

Is there parking?

Valet parking is available, so arriving is one less thing to think about.
