Signature Facials & Targeted Treatments

Anti-Acne Back Treatment

Made for breakouts on the back, this treatment works toward clearer, smoother skin there. I begin by looking closely at where your back is congested, oily or inflamed, then deep-cleanse to lift away impurities and excess oil. Specialized exfoliation follows to help clear clogged pores, and a soothing mask calms inflammation and hydrates. It finishes with a calming massage that eases the tension so many of us carry in the upper back.

Pressure, timing and products are adjusted to your comfort and your skin’s sensitivity, never a single uniform routine. How many sessions you need depends on your skin and how it responds, so I’ll suggest a plan after your first assessment rather than a fixed number.

What it includes

  • An assessment of congestion, oiliness and inflammation
  • Deep cleansing to remove impurities and excess oil
  • Specialized exfoliation to help unclog pores
  • A soothing mask to calm inflammation and hydrate
  • A calming massage to relieve tension
  • Aftercare guidance to support your skin at home

Good to know

  • Arrive with your back free of heavy lotions or oils, and wear loose clothing afterwards.
  • Downtime is minimal. Some temporary redness or sensitivity is possible, so tell me if you have an event coming up and I’ll help you time it.

Clinical Peels & Skin Resurfacing

Dermaplaning

Dermaplaning uses a specialized surgical scalpel to gently skim the surface of your skin. With controlled, precise strokes I lift away dead skin and vellus hair, the “stuck” layer that a gritty scrub at home tends to miss while overworking your skin. You leave with skin that feels instantly softer and reflects light better, not raw or over-exfoliated.

With that surface layer cleared, your serums and moisturizers absorb more evenly, and makeup blends instead of catching on peach fuzz or dry patches. It’s a strong choice for rough texture, dullness, makeup that won’t behave, or skin you want smooth up close for photos, a wedding or a night out. It’s often a perfect entry point, with peels as a next step when your skin is ready.

Good to know

  • Results typically last three to six weeks. Many of my regulars book it as a monthly reset.
  • There’s little to no downtime, though mild redness can stay for a few hours to a day.
  • Before an event, book three to seven days ahead. If it’s your first time or your skin is sensitive, lean closer to a week.
  • It won’t make facial hair grow back thicker or darker. It removes hair at the surface and doesn’t change the follicle.
  • It isn’t recommended over active cystic acne or after recent isotretinoin use. If acne is flaring, I’ll talk it through with you and choose a safer plan.

Virtual Skin Journey Consultation

Your journey begins with a comprehensive one-on-one consultation. I take a close look at your skin, your concerns, your current routine, your lifestyle and your goals, then build a plan you can follow at home.

I assess your skin over live video and from any photos you send, talking through texture, oiliness, sensitivity and anything visible that worries you, along with seasonal factors like cold, dry winters and humid summers. Afterwards you have a personalized Skin Journey plan: recommended products, step-by-step instructions, and when to introduce each one, explained in plain language.

What it includes

  • Virtual skin assessment
  • Review of your current skincare products and routine
  • Discussion of your primary skin concerns and goals
  • Personalized skincare education
  • Customized home-care routine
  • Professional product recommendations
  • Guidance on how and when to introduce recommended products
  • Personalized Skin Journey plan

Good to know

  • Come prepared with a list of your current products and how often you use them, recent photos taken in natural light, and your questions.
  • A virtual consultation can’t replace hands-on treatment or a medical diagnosis. If you need an in-person visit or a referral, I’ll tell you when that step makes sense.
  • Your photos and session details are used only for your assessment and plan, and are treated as confidential.

Virtual Skin Journey Follow-up

Your follow-up is where I see how your skin is responding to your plan and decide whether your routine needs adjusting.

I compare what you’ve noticed, your photos and your product use against where you started, looking at tolerance, irritation and whether improvement is steady. Changes are prioritized: one or two small adjustments first, bigger steps only if they’re needed, and each one comes with a reason and what to watch for. You leave knowing which products to keep, swap or stop, and what comes next.

What it includes

  • Skin progress review
  • Review of your current routine
  • Discussion of product response
  • Routine adjustments when appropriate
  • Updated recommendations and next steps

Good to know

  • Bring a list of every product you’re using, notes on how your skin has responded, and recent photos if you have them.
  • It’s ideal for monitoring and planning. It isn’t a substitute for hands-on treatment.

Teen Virtual Consultation (14–18 yrs old)

For teens aged 14 to 18 dealing with acne, dryness or uneven texture. I look at the photos you send ahead of time, then talk through what your teen sees in the mirror and what’s already been tried. This isn’t a random product list and a “good luck”.

I tighten up the basics first (cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen) and then add acne steps that suit the skin type and comfort level. Acne usually improves by doing fewer things, more consistently, so I build a routine a teen will actually keep up on busy mornings. It also suits melanin-rich skin, with advice that respects how dark skin can react to irritation. If anything sounds beyond what non-prescription skincare should handle, I’ll say so plainly and recommend a dermatologist.

Good to know

  • Parents are welcome to join, or teens can meet with me privately if that feels better.
  • Send clear, makeup-free photos in natural light: straight-on, left side, right side, and a close-up of the area that bothers you most. Skip the filters.
  • Many teens notice early changes in two to four weeks, like less redness and fewer new breakouts. Deeper improvement often takes eight to twelve weeks.
  • Skincare products are purchased separately and aren’t included in the consultation.
